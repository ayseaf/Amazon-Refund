# Dashboard

## Scope

The local application shell and its seven sections: Dashboard, Amazon Orders, Price Monitoring, Refund Assistant, Returns and Deadlines, Savings History, and Settings.

## Mode

operate

## Audience

Adam, at a Mac, checking whether a past purchase is cheaper now and whether a return window is close.

## Job

Read purchase totals, the latest checked total, the difference, and the return deadline, then decide what to do personally. The app does not act.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A local ledger you can scan before you notice the ticket-counter colors. It refuses a hero row of metric cards and it refuses to dress itself as Amazon.

OWN-WORLD: Burgundy rail #8C1D33 and slip text #FFF6F6, shirt-tissue ground #E4F0EA, ink #1A2420, slip surface #FFFCFB. Barlow for interface text, Barlow Condensed for money and the stamp. The signature is a claim stamp on each difference: dashed when unchecked, solid when checked, doubled when the drop meets the savings threshold.

STORY: The visitor sees which labeled totals are lower, which return windows are inside 7 days, and that Amazon has not been contacted and no refund has been submitted.

Paid, latest, and difference share one row. A right-hand margin holds the guarantee, the last synchronization, and Check prices. On a narrow window the navigation becomes a single scrolling row and the margin stacks under the table.
